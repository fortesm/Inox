## How to read it

- **Name comparisons**: codegen deciding what a call or name is by its
  text. Each becomes a TIR node or a `Builtin` operation the analysis
  chose (step 3), or a place (step 4: `__member`, `__index`, `__slice`,
  `__set`, `__check`, `__pure`).
- **Lookups by AST node**: codegen asking `SemanticResult` about an AST
  node (directly, or through the emitter's wrappers `typeOf`,
  `resolvedConstant`, `intrinsicCallOf`). Each becomes a property of the
  TIR node (steps 2-4).
- **AST shape tests**: codegen testing an AST node's kind. They go when
  codegen walks the TIR (step 5); `codegen/AstQueries.cpp` holds 45 of them.
- `semantics_.types()` (the type context) is not listed: codegen reading
  `types::Type` objects is its job, not a decision.
